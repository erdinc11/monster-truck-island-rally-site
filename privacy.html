Monster Truck — Island Rally

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Summary

Monster Truck — Island Rally does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The game does not require an account or an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The game stores local gameplay records and settings on your device so that your rally progress and preferences can be retained. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account information.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, tracking, or crash-reporting data.
  • No payment information or in-app purchase data.

Third-party services

The current version does not use third-party analytics, advertising, authentication, or data collection services.

Children’s privacy

Because the game does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to aysoymetin@gmail.com.
